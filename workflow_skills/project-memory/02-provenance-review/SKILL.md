# Provenance review

End with a `## Sources` section. Include every supplied `artifact_ref` exactly as provided, together
with its workflow name and run ID. Every factual section must be supportable by one of those source
references. If sources conflict, state the conflict instead of choosing silently.


Preserve the founder's goal, explicit corrections and current product positioning with their
sources. New reports do not automatically overrule founder decisions or established product
facts. Retain useful older evidence, distinguish observations from recommendations, and record
why a correction supersedes a claim. Analytics belongs in dated evidence, not timeless facts.
